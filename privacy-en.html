Privacy policy – PDF Complete

Last updated: November 5, 2026

PDF Complete ("the app") is a free PDF reader and editor developed by Full 4ever. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the app.

1. Your documents stay on your phone

Opening, reading, AI summaries and AI tools, text recognition (OCR), signatures, stamps, added text and erasing all happen entirely on your device. The app does not upload your documents, signatures or extracted text to any of the developer's servers.

2. Data stored on the device

The app stores on your device: the history of opened documents, favorites and bookmarks, the signatures you create, copies of edited PDFs, undo/redo versions, thumbnails, the page where you left off and your settings (for example the language). This data never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use "Clear data" in Android settings.

3. Internet connections

The app connects to the Internet only to: (a) download, once and only if you ask for it, the offline artificial intelligence model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), without sending personal data; (b) occasionally show you the Google Play review prompt. If you choose Gemini Nano, the model is managed by Android (AICore) and runs on the device.

4. Permissions

Camera: only when you use the scanner or photograph your signature; photos are processed on the device. File access: on Android 10 and earlier, to show the PDFs on your phone on the Home screen; on newer versions the app only sees the files you open yourself, from the system picker or from other apps. Internet and network state: to download the AI model.

5. Reports

If you use "Report a bug" or "Report" under an AI answer, your email app opens with a pre-filled message: you decide whether to send it. We only receive what is in the email (for example the text of the reported answer, the phone model and the Android version) and use it only to fix the problem.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Legal basis and your rights

The app contains no ads and the developer does not collect personal data. You still have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object, and to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it) or the authority in your country.

8. Contact

Full 4ever — full4ever.support+pdf@gmail.com

9. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with the date of the update.
